## 📂 Dataset Used
- Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV)

### Dataset contains:
- Sales
- Profit
- Region
- Category
- Segment
- Discount
- Order Date
- Customer Details
  
## 📈 Dashboard Overview
This interactive Power BI dashboard analyzes sales and profit performance of a retail superstore business.

The dashboard helps identify:
- Sales trends over time
- Most profitable regions
- Best performing product categories
- Impact of discounts on profit
- Customer segment contribution

## 📊 Dashboard Visuals

1️⃣ KPI Cards
The dashboard includes three KPI cards:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

2️⃣ Sales Trend Over Time
- Visual Type: Line Chart
- Purpose: Analyze yearly sales growth trends.

Insight:
Sales increased significantly after 2015, showing business growth over time.

3️⃣ Sales by Category
- Visual Type: Bar Chart
- Purpose: Compare sales across product categories.

Insight:
Technology category generated the highest sales among all categories.

4️⃣ Profit by Region
- Visual Type: Column Chart
- Purpose: Compare profit generated by different regions.

Insight:
West region produced the highest profit.

 5️⃣ Discount Impact on Profit
- Visual Type: Scatter Chart
- Purpose: Analyze relationship between discount and profit.

Insight:
Higher discounts negatively affected profitability.

6️⃣ Sales by Segment
- Visual Type: Donut Chart
- Purpose: Analyze contribution of customer segments.

Insight:
Consumer segment contributed the highest sales revenue.

📌 Key Business Insights

- Technology products perform best in sales.
- West region is the most profitable region.
- Excessive discounts reduce profit margins.
- Consumer customers contribute maximum revenue.
